Hisn Al-Muslim

Your companion for daily Azkar and Salah reminders, ensuring you stay connected to your spiritual practices.

Key Features

Azkar Notifications

Receive random Azkar notifications at specified times to keep your heart and mind connected to Allah.

Salah Reminders

Get notified about Salah times dynamically based on your timezone, ensuring you never miss a prayer.

Customizable Settings

Easily configure Azkar and Salah times, and choose whether to notify all users or specific groups.

About the Module

The Hisn Al-Muslim module is designed to help Muslims stay connected to their daily spiritual practices. It provides automated reminders for Azkar and Salah, ensuring you never miss an opportunity to remember Allah. This module is a form of "Zakat al-Ilm" (charity through knowledge) and is free to use and contribute to.

Contribute & Collaborate

This module is open source and welcomes contributions from the community. Whether you want to report issues, suggest improvements, or add new features, your input is highly valued.
